# Padhaaro Sa.. Travel CRM — Complete Product Description

*The all-in-one business management platform built for travel agencies, tour operators, and hospitality companies.*

## Introduction: Why Travel Businesses Need More Than Spreadsheets

Running a travel business is one of the most detail-heavy jobs in the world. A single customer enquiry can turn into a fourteen-day itinerary touching five cities, three hotels, two flights, a safari, a desert camp, a dozen payments, a visa application, and a hundred WhatsApp messages. Multiply that by every family, honeymoon couple, corporate group, and wedding party you serve in a season, and the picture becomes clear: the travel business runs on details, and details are exactly what spreadsheets, notebooks, and memory lose first.

Most travel agencies today operate across a scattered set of tools — enquiries in WhatsApp, itineraries in Word files, invoices in Excel, hotel contacts in a phone book, payments in a diary, and social media in someone's personal account. Every handover between these tools is a chance for something to slip: a follow-up forgotten, a balance never collected, a visa expiring two days before departure, a hotel double-booked.

**Padhaaro Sa.. Travel CRM** was built to end that scatter. It is a single, beautifully designed workspace where every part of a travel business lives together — from the first "hello" of a new lead to the final thank-you note on a paid invoice, from a tour package in your catalog to the tiger safari on day three of an itinerary, from your best-performing sales agent to your Instagram content calendar. One login, one screen, one source of truth.

The platform carries the warmth of its origin in its design. Inspired by the terracotta of Jaipur's Hawa Mahal, the deep plum of royal Rajasthani textiles, and the gold of desert sunsets, the interface feels less like enterprise software and more like a well-kept haveli — organised, welcoming, and unmistakably yours. Your logo sits on every screen and every printed document. When a customer receives a quotation from you, it looks like it came from a premium brand, because it did.

## The Dashboard: Your Business at a Glance

Every morning begins at the dashboard — a single screen that answers the question every owner asks: *"How is my business doing today?"*

The top row shows the four numbers that matter most: **Revenue Collected** (every rupee received, across all payment modes), **Outstanding Dues** (money billed but not yet in the bank, with a count of pending invoices), **Pipeline Value** (the total worth of every active enquiry you're pursuing), and **Upcoming Trips** (departures on the horizon, along with your win rate — the percentage of enquiries you convert into bookings).

Below the numbers, the picture deepens. A six-month **revenue trend chart** shows whether the business is climbing or cooling. The **sales pipeline** breaks your leads into stages — New, Contacted, Quoted, Negotiation, Won — so you can see exactly where deals are stalling. **Upcoming Departures** lists the next trips leaving, each with destination, traveller count, customer name, and a live countdown ("11 days to go"). **Follow-ups Due** surfaces every promised call and pending task so no lead ever goes cold — items due today, tomorrow, and overdue items flagged in red.

The dashboard also reaches beyond sales. **Upcoming Events** previews the weddings, corporate offsites, and group departures your events team is managing. **Social Reach** rolls up your follower counts across Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, YouTube, X, and LinkedIn, with growth indicators for each channel. **Recent Invoices** shows the latest billing activity with live payment status.

Nothing on this screen is decoration. Every number is computed live from your actual data, and every card is a doorway — click a departure to open the booking, click a follow-up to open the lead, click an invoice to see the payment history.

## Leads & Contacts: The Pipeline That Never Forgets

Every travel business lives or dies by its enquiries. The Leads module treats each one as precious.

**Capture everything.** A lead records the customer's name, phone, email, and company, the enquiry source (Website, Referral, WhatsApp, Instagram, Walk-in, or Phone), the destination they're dreaming of, intended travel dates, number of travellers, and their budget. Priority flags (High / Medium / Low) keep your attention where the money is.

**The drag-and-drop pipeline board.** Leads appear as cards on a kanban board with columns for each stage. Move a lead from "Contacted" to "Quoted" by simply dragging its card — the change saves instantly. Each column shows its total count and combined budget value, so you can see at a glance that, say, ₹25 lakh worth of business is sitting in Negotiation. Prefer rows to cards? Switch to the list view with one click, filter by stage, and search by name, phone, or destination.

**A complete relationship timeline.** Open any lead and you'll find their entire history: every call logged, every email noted, every WhatsApp exchanged, every meeting held. Add follow-up tasks with due dates; overdue ones turn red and surface on the dashboard. One-tap buttons let you call the customer or open WhatsApp directly from their profile.

**Everything connected.** From the lead's page you can raise a quotation, create a booking, or generate an invoice — and each of those documents stays linked back to the lead forever. Six months later, when the same family calls again, their whole story is one search away.

When a deal is lost, record *why* — a cheaper competitor, changed plans, budget mismatch — and that intelligence accumulates into a picture of what your market really wants.

## Bookings & Itineraries: From Confirmation to Bon Voyage

The moment an enquiry becomes a confirmed trip, the Bookings module takes over.

Each booking receives an automatic reference number (PDH-2026-0001 style) and carries the trip's essentials: title, destination, start and end dates, adults and children, package amount, assigned agent, and status — Draft, Confirmed, Ongoing, Completed, or Cancelled. Bookings display as elegant cards showing nights, traveller count, days-to-departure countdowns, and customer details.

**The day-by-day itinerary builder** is where trips come alive. For each day of the journey you record the day's title, date, location, hotel, meal plan, transport, and a free-form description of activities and sightseeing. Days render as a beautiful vertical timeline — numbered medallions connected by a line, each day showing its hotel with an icon, meals, and transport at a glance. Add days as the plan grows; delete one and the rest renumber automatically. The result reads like a professional tour dossier, ready to walk a customer through.

Bookings link to invoices, so the money side of every trip is one click from the operational side. Change a trip's status with a single tap on the status bar — no forms, no friction.

## Quotations: Proposals That Win

A quotation is often the first "document" a customer ever receives from you — and first impressions close deals.

The quote builder assembles professional, GST-ready proposals in minutes. Add line items with descriptions, quantities, and rates; the system computes the subtotal, applies flat discounts, calculates GST at your chosen rate, and presents a grand total — all updating live as you type. Add notes ("Rates valid for travel before 31 March") and terms ("50% advance to confirm"). Set an issue date and a validity window.

The finished quotation is a branded document: your logo and company details in a rich plum-and-maroon header, the customer's details, an itemised table, and your thank-you line in the footer. Print it, download it as a pixel-perfect PDF, or share it on WhatsApp.

Quotes carry status — Draft, Sent, Accepted, Rejected, Expired — and the list view totals your open value and accepted value so you always know what's on the table. And when a customer says yes, one click on **"To Invoice"** converts the entire quotation — every line item, the discount, the tax rate, the terms — into a ready invoice. No retyping, no transcription errors.

If you're quoting a brand-new customer, you don't need to create their contact first: type their name into the quote form and the CRM creates the contact automatically, linked and ready.

## Invoices & Payments: Every Rupee Accounted For

Billing is where good software pays for itself.

Invoices share the same polished, branded design as quotations, with automatic numbering (INV-2026-0001) and full GST computation. Each invoice can link to its booking, carries due dates, and — uniquely useful — lets you **choose per invoice whether to print your bank details**. Show the bank box (bank name, account number, IFSC, UPI ID pulled from your settings) on pending invoices; hide it on fully-paid ones.

**Part-payments are first-class citizens.** Travel businesses rarely collect in one shot — an advance on confirmation, a second instalment, a balance before departure. Record each payment with its amount, mode (UPI, Cash, Card, Bank Transfer, Cheque), date, and transaction reference. The invoice recalculates automatically: paid amount, balance due, and status — Unpaid, Partial, Paid, or Overdue — with no manual bookkeeping. The printed invoice lists every payment received, so customers see a transparent ledger.

The invoices list doubles as your receivables report: total invoiced, total collected, total outstanding, and a count of overdue invoices that need chasing — each with the customer's face and a click-through to the full document.

Both invoices and quotes export as true PDFs — sized to their content (no awkward half-empty pages), rendered in full brand colour, correct in every PDF viewer, generated on your own machine with no external service.

## Travel Management: The Eleven-Module Operations Suite

Beyond sales and billing lies the operational heart of a tour operator — the catalog and logistics that make trips actually happen. Padhaaro Sa.. CRM organises this into eleven dedicated modules, every one with search, filters, live KPIs, and instant add/edit/delete.

**1. Tour Packages.** Your product catalog. Each package records its code, category (Domestic / International), style (Group, Customized, Fixed Departure, Honeymoon, Religious, Adventure, Corporate, Seasonal), destinations, nights and days, starting price, availability (Available / Limited / Sold Out), plus highlights, inclusions, and exclusions. KPIs show your total tours, active count, international count, and average price.

**2. Destinations.** A structured gazetteer of everywhere you operate — countries, states, cities, forts, temples, beaches, hill stations, and wildlife reserves — each with its best travel season and a description your team can quote from.

**3. Hotels.** Your accommodation inventory: star ratings, categories from Budget to Heritage, meal plans (EP/CP/MAP/AP), per-night tariffs, total and available room counts, and direct phone numbers. The dashboard KPI computes your room availability across all properties.

**4. Flights.** Track every air booking: airline, flight number, PNR, route, departure and arrival, seats, baggage allowance, and live status — Confirmed, Pending, Cancelled, or Delayed.

**5. Transport & Fleet.** Every vehicle — sedans, SUVs, tempo travellers, buses, luxury coaches — with registration numbers, seating capacity, per-day pricing, GPS flags, and assigned drivers with their phone numbers. Status tracking shows what's Available, On Trip, or in Maintenance.

**6. Activities & Sightseeing.** The experiences that make itineraries sing: monument visits, hot-air balloons, camel safaris, cultural evenings, ziplines, museum tickets — each with duration, city, and per-person price.

**7. Tour Guides.** Your guide roster with languages spoken, base city, years of experience, specialisation (heritage, photography, culinary…), daily rates, star ratings, and availability.

**8. Travelers.** Passenger management done properly: passport numbers and expiry dates, visa status workflow (Not Required → Applied → Approved / Rejected), nationality, emergency contacts, dietary and special requests, and confidential medical notes.

**9. Vendors & Suppliers.** Every partner — hotels, airlines, transporters, restaurants, activity providers, local partners — with contact people, contract status (Active / Pending / Expired), ratings, and, critically, **outstanding payables**, so you always know what you owe and to whom.

**10. Travel Documents.** A registry for passports, visas, tickets, hotel vouchers, insurance policies, permits, and agreements — with reference numbers, owners, issue and expiry dates, and a status that flags what's Expiring before it becomes an airport emergency.

**11. Reviews & Feedback.** Capture every review, complaint, and suggestion with star ratings and NPS scores. Track resolution on complaints and let your average rating become a marketing asset.

## Events & MICE: Big Days, Handled

Destination weddings, corporate offsites, festival group departures — events are the highest-stakes, highest-margin work a travel company does. The Events module manages each one with its type (Wedding, Corporate, MICE, Festival, Group Tour), venue, dates, guest count, and budget, moving through Planning → Confirmed → Ongoing → Completed.

Each event carries a **team assignment board**: attach your staff with defined roles — Lead Planner, Coordinator, Logistics, Hospitality — so on the big day everyone knows exactly what they own.

## Travel Calendar: Time, Unified

The calendar gathers every date in your business onto one agenda: trip departures and returns, flight schedules, event dates, follow-up deadlines, and document expiries. Each entry is colour-coded by type, grouped by day, labelled with relative time ("Tomorrow", "in 12 days"), and clickable through to its source record. Filter to just departures, just flights, or just expiring documents. It is the operational heartbeat of the company on a single page.

## Team & Employees: People and Performance

Your team page presents every staff member as a rich profile card: designation, department, location, contact details, and joining date. Live counters show each person's leads, trips, and event assignments. Most powerfully, each member carries a **monthly revenue target** with a progress bar computed from their actual booked value — green when they've crossed it, terracotta when on track, amber when they need help. A trophy marks your top performer, and team-wide KPIs total your headcount, departments, and combined revenue.

## Social Media: Marketing in the Same House

Uniquely among CRMs at this level, Padhaaro Sa.. brings your social presence inside. Connect your Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, YouTube, X, and LinkedIn accounts — handles, profile links, follower counts, growth and engagement rates — and see them as branded channel cards with live totals on your dashboard.

The **content calendar** manages your posts across all channels: draft, schedule, and mark published, with campaign tags and engagement metrics (likes, comments, shares, reach) on published content. A **Share Now** menu opens any platform's own composer pre-filled with your caption — WhatsApp, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Telegram, or email — plus one-tap caption copying. Connected Facebook pages support **direct API publishing** from inside the CRM.

Best of all, marketing connects to inventory: every tour package and destination has a share button that auto-writes a social caption from its details — price, duration, highlights, hashtags — and drops it into your content calendar as a ready draft. Your catalog becomes your content engine.

## Design, Technology & Trust

The entire experience is **fully responsive** — the same power on a phone at a hotel front desk as on the office desktop, with tables that scroll gracefully and dialogs that fit every screen. The interface is fast: instant search, filter chips that keep your scroll position, drag-and-drop that saves in the background.

Under the hood, the platform is built on a modern, battle-tested stack — Next.js and React with a real relational database through Prisma — running entirely on **your own machine**. Your customer list, your financials, your pricing: none of it sits on a third-party server. There are no per-user fees, no subscriptions, and no internet dependency for daily work. When you're ready to go multi-user online, the same codebase deploys to the cloud with a database swap.

Company branding is fully configurable — name, tagline, logo, address, GSTIN, bank details, UPI ID, and the thank-you line on your documents — all editable from the Settings page and reflected instantly across every screen and every PDF.
